120KM/H

Why 120?

120 is flat out. Top speed, the maximum extension of a polo pony.

It is the moment when horse and rider become one. That is the feeling we want every guest to take home, whether it is your first ride or your hundredth chukker.

Meet

R. Enrique Diaz

With a passion for horses since the age of eleven, Enrique has travelled the world as a professional polo player, groom and trainer, from Hawaii to Santa Barbara.

He does it all: he trains the horses, teaches players of every level and built Paradise Ranch by hand. Now he shares it with guests from all over the world.

Welcome to

Mar Chiquita

A coastal lagoon where the pampas meet the Atlantic, a short drive from Mar del Plata. Quiet beaches, wide skies and one of the richest bird habitats in Argentina.

  • Birdwatching on the lagoon
  • Fishing and kitesurfing
  • Beaches and sunsets over the water
  • Close to Mar del Plata
